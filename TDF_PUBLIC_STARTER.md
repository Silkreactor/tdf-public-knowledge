# TDF — public starter for your AI

Edition 0.1 · 19 October 2026.

This is a small public source guide for Traditional Dream Factory (TDF). It
contains three reference links and a reading prompt. It does not contain the
linked pages or a comprehensive body of verified TDF facts.

## Public references

- **P001 — Project introduction:** https://www.traditionaldreamfactory.com/
- **P002 — Ecology:** https://www.traditionaldreamfactory.com/pages/ecology

does not establish the accuracy of every statement or grant rights to copy or
republish their contents. Published descriptions and changing terms remain the
responsibility of their publishers.

## Prompt to use with this file

Use the public references above to answer my question about TDF. If you can
browse, read the relevant page and cite its URL and the section supporting each
important claim, with the date accessed. Distinguish the publisher's statements
from independently verified facts, your interpretation and unresolved questions.
Do not invent current schedules, availability, rights, appointments or decisions.
If you cannot open a source, say which one and ask for a relevant public excerpt.
Do not treat a list of links as evidence that you have read the linked content.

Start with a short answer and let me ask follow-up questions. If I suggest a
correction, treat it as a proposal needing a source and review.

My question: **What would you like to understand about TDF?**
